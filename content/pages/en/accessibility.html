We believe in providing equal service to every visitor, and we work to make this website available, convenient and easy to use for people with disabilities, in line with the Israeli Equal Rights for Persons with Disabilities (Service Accessibility Adjustments) Regulations, 2013, and Israeli Standard 5568, based on WCAG 2.0 level AA.

What we've done

  • The site uses semantic code, with ordered headings, navigation landmarks and ARIA attributes where needed.
  • You can navigate with the keyboard alone (Tab, Shift+Tab, Enter, Esc), including a "skip to content" link and a visible focus indicator.
  • Images have alternative text, and form fields have linked labels.
  • Sufficient colour contrast and readable fonts; you can zoom up to 200% in your browser without losing content.
  • The site adapts to phones and tablets and respects the "reduce motion" system setting.
