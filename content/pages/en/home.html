Our story

It all began with a taste of childhood

After moving to Israel from Montreal, Valerie Sarfati couldn't find the smoked salmon she grew up with. So she went back to Canada, learned the craft from Frank – a Greek master salmon smoker and local legend – and came home with the secret.

Today, together with her son Yaakov, she smokes salmon at the family smokehouse in Shilat for thousands of families across Israel. Every piece passes through their hands and every slice is cut with care – because there are no shortcuts to flavour.
