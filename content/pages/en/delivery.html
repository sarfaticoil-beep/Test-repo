Frequently asked questions

How do the products stay cold on the way?
Orders are carefully packed and leave in a refrigerated delivery van on your area's delivery day.
How do I pay?
Once you've sent your order, we call you back to confirm availability and the final weight, and to take payment (by phone or another method agreed with you).
Why can the final price change?
Some products (whole fillets, hot-smoked fish, etc.) are sold by weight. Every fish weighs a little differently, so you are charged by actual weight – and we confirm it with you before payment.
How long do the products keep?
The use-by date is printed on every pack. Vacuum-packed smoked fish keeps in the fridge at 0–4°C; once opened, eat within a few days.
