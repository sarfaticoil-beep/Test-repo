It began with a taste

From Montreal to Shilat

After moving to Israel, Valerie Sarfati missed the smoked salmon she used to enjoy in Montreal, the city where she grew up. The taste of home stayed with her, but whatever she tried, she couldn't find it in Israel.

Valerie didn't give up. She called her father in Canada to find the secret, and he put her in touch with one man: Frank – a Greek fishmonger, master salmon smoker and a true legend.

Valerie travelled to Montreal and trained with Frank, who generously shared the recipe, the techniques and the methods. Whole days of smoke, focus and handwork – all to get the flavour exactly right.

Our journey

  1. The beginning

    Valerie returns from Montreal as a smoked salmon chef and starts smoking at home – small, with love and persistence – until the taste is just right.

  2. 2015

    SARFATI, a gourmet delights company, is founded. Valerie shyly starts selling her salmon on social media – and the demand soon follows.

  3. Now it's a family

    Yaakov, Valerie's son, joins the business out of love for his mother's salmon and leads production and the team.

  4. 2022

    Valerie and Yaakov open a smokehouse and deli in the Shilat industrial zone – and later a branch at Sarona Market in Tel Aviv.

Our products, our pride

No shortcuts to flavour

Every salmon is smoked the traditional way, over real wood, just like in the old days. Every piece passes through our hands and every slice is cut precisely – paper-thin, almost translucent, bright orange.

  • Choice raw materials – every fish carefully selected, with freshness our top priority.
  • Hygiene and safety – our smokehouse is supervised by the Ministry of Health and meets high standards of production and preservation.
  • Kashrut – most products are under Badatz Eda Haredit supervision, and some under Mehadrin Hevel Modiin. Details
  • We listen – we're happy to meet special requests, including allergies and kashrut requirements.
